## Controls

- **Move Character:** Left Stick (Controller) / `WASD` (Keyboard)
- **Interact with Objects:** Button South (`A` on Xbox) / `E`
- **Cast Spell:** Button East (`X` on Xbox) / `F`
- **Pause Game:** `Escape` / Start Button (Controller)
