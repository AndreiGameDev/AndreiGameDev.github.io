After an honest day's work, a weary lumberjack steps through what he thought was his cabin door—only to find himself in an ever-shifting, monster-infested dungeon. With no exit in sight, he must fight through endless cycles, utilizing his trusty axe to survive a loop that never ends.
[Download here](https://andreibulletdev.itch.io/mysterylumberjack)
## Controls
- **Move** → `WASD`
- **Attack** → `Left Click`
