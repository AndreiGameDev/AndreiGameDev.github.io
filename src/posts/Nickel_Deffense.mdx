# Nickel Defense

A strategy game where you must protect the **nickel coin** from being dragged away by magnets by strategically placing towers.

[Download here](https://github.com/AndreiGameDev/NickelDeffense/releases/tag/Release)
---

## Gameplay Instructions
- **Placing Towers** → Pull the menu from the right, select a tower, and click on the **green tiles** to place it.
- **Upgrading Towers** → Click on a tower to upgrade it.
- **Upgrading the Factory** → Click on the factory to upgrade it.

---

## Controls

### General Controls
- **Press `T`** → Toggle mouse while in Camera Mode.

### Camera Controls
- **`W A S D`** → Move the camera
- **`CTRL`** → Descend
- **`Space`** → Ascend
